2026

Econometrics

Cattolica University — Milan, Italy

Content summary
  1. Linear regression models: specification, matrix notation, simulation, and estimation.
  2. Geometry of least squares: projection methods, Frisch–Waugh–Lovell theorem, goodness of fit, and influential observations.
  3. Statistical properties of OLS: unbiasedness, consistency, variance–covariance matrix, estimation accuracy, and model misspecification.
  4. Hypothesis testing in linear regression: single and multiple restrictions, finite-sample and asymptotic inference.
  5. Confidence intervals and robust inference: heteroskedasticity-robust and heteroskedasticity-and-autocorrelation-consistent covariance estimation.
  6. Bootstrap methods: parametric and semiparametric bootstrap.
  7. Instrumental variables: measurement error, simultaneous equations, identification, consistency, and IV estimation.
  8. Generalized least squares and extensions: heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation testing, ARMA models, and panel data models (fixed effects, random effects, and between-groups estimation).
2025

Financial Data Science

Bocconi University — Milan, Italy

Content summary
  1. Foundations of time series analysis: forecasting principles and decision theory.
  2. AR, MA, and ARMA models: model selection, maximum likelihood estimation, and forecasting.
  3. Stationarity, unit roots, and spurious regressions.
  4. Volatility modeling: ARCH and GARCH models.
  5. Advanced univariate volatility models: non-Gaussian innovations, exogenous variables, estimation, inference, and forecasting.
  6. Realized variance.
  7. Machine learning methods for finance: decision trees and random forests, support vector machines (classification and regression), artificial neural networks.
2025

Macroeconomics

Cattolica University — Milan, Italy

Content summary
  1. Macroeconomic aggregates: GDP, national accounts, price indices, monetary aggregates, and public debt.
  2. The long run: aggregate demand and supply, growth, savings and investment, inflation.
  3. The short run: AD–AS model, Keynesian framework, fiscal multipliers, IS-MP model.
  4. The medium run: Phillips curve, inflation expectations, NAIRU, steady state dynamics.
  5. Market imperfections and institutions in macroeconomic equilibrium.
  6. Macroeconomic policy: monetary and fiscal policy, debt sustainability, exchange rate regimes.
  7. Financial crises and recent macroeconomic developments.
